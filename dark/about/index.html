About

Built on trust,
run by people with

A production service company built to do one thing at a high level: bring foreign production to Canada and get it made.

Unlike most Canadian production companies, we don’t represent directors. That means no house roster, no competing agenda and no reason to steer your production anywhere but where it belongs. You bring the director and the idea. We bring the right people, places and production expertise to make it happen.
